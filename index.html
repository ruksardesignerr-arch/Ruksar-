Our Story

More Than Just Mehndi Art

For over a decade, our studio has turned mehndi into a language of love -- adorning brides on the most important day of their lives. What began as a family passion has grown into a trusted bridal studio known for patience, precision and designs that photograph beautifully and age even better.

We believe your mehndi should feel like you: whether that is a full-hand heirloom canvas or a whisper of fine florals, every session is unhurried, personal and entirely yours.

  • Hand-drawn, never stamped

    Every line is drawn freehand -- no stencils, no shortcuts.

  • Organic, skin-kind henna

    Freshly mixed cones from natural henna, eucalyptus and clove.

  • A calm, pampered day

    Studio or at-home sessions, paced around your comfort.

-- The Ruksar Designer Studio
